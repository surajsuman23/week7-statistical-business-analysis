Business data : business_data.csv (500 customers)
Sales data    : data/sales_data.csv (100 orders)
Significance  : alpha = 0.05 (two-sided)

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
T1: Do churned customers have shorter tenure?
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Test        : Welch two-sample t-test
H0          : Mean Tenure is the same for Churned and Retained customers
H1          : Mean Tenure differs between Churned and Retained customers
Statistic   : -34.5349
p-value     : 8.98e-130
Effect size : Cohen's d = -1.918
Decision    : REJECT H0 - significant
Conclusion  : Reject H0. Mean Tenure is 6.0 for Churned vs 40.2 for Retained (difference
              -34.2); effect size is large. Mann-Whitney U agrees (p = 2.00e-28).

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
T2: Do churned customers pay higher monthly charges?
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Test        : Welch two-sample t-test
H0          : Mean MonthlyCharges is the same for Churned and Retained customers
H1          : Mean MonthlyCharges differs between Churned and Retained customers
Statistic   : 2.6464
p-value     : 0.0101
Effect size : Cohen's d = 0.350
Decision    : REJECT H0 - significant
Conclusion  : Reject H0. Mean MonthlyCharges is 129.8 for Churned vs 111.7 for Retained
              (difference +18.1); effect size is small. Mann-Whitney U agrees (p =
              0.0183).

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
T3: Is churn related to contract type?
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Test        : Chi-square test of independence
H0          : Contract and Churn are independent
H1          : Contract and Churn are associated
Statistic   : 27.7153
p-value     : 9.59e-07
Effect size : Cramer's V = 0.235
Decision    : REJECT H0 - significant
Conclusion  : Reject H0. Contract and Churn are associated (Cramer's V = 0.24, small).
              Churn rate: Month-to-month 20.6%, One year 4.3%, Two year 6.9%.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
T4: Is churn related to payment method?
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Test        : Chi-square test of independence
H0          : PaymentMethod and Churn are independent
H1          : PaymentMethod and Churn are associated
Statistic   : 3.8695
p-value     : 0.1445
Effect size : Cramer's V = 0.088
Decision    : FAIL TO REJECT H0 - not significant
Conclusion  : Fail to reject H0. PaymentMethod and Churn are not shown to be associated
              (Cramer's V = 0.09, negligible). Churn rate: Bank Transfer 6.9%, Credit
              Card 13.5%, Electronic Check 11.0%.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
T5: Do monthly charges differ by contract type?
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Test        : One-way ANOVA
H0          : Mean MonthlyCharges is equal across all Contract groups
H1          : At least one Contract group has a different mean MonthlyCharges
Statistic   : 0.0313
p-value     : 0.9692
Effect size : eta squared = 0.000
Decision    : FAIL TO REJECT H0 - not significant
Conclusion  : Fail to reject H0. Group means: Month-to-month 112.8, One year 114.0, Two
              year 114.1. eta^2 = 0.000 (negligible). Kruskal-Wallis agrees (p =
              0.9570).

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
T6: Does order value differ by product?
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Test        : One-way ANOVA
H0          : Mean Total_Sales is equal across all Product groups
H1          : At least one Product group has a different mean Total_Sales
Statistic   : 2.0508
p-value     : 0.0934
Effect size : eta squared = 0.079
Decision    : FAIL TO REJECT H0 - not significant
Conclusion  : Fail to reject H0. Group means: Headphones 92,268.9, Laptop 162,050.4,
              Monitor 89,871.4, Phone 142,969.7, Tablet 110,936.2. eta^2 = 0.079
              (medium). Kruskal-Wallis agrees (p = 0.1082).
